01 · RPO

Recruitment Process Outsourcing

"Your talent function, fully managed."Get started

Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO) means we become an extension of your HR team, owning the entire hiring lifecycle from workforce planning and sourcing through to onboarding. You get the expertise of a dedicated recruitment function without the overhead of building one in-house.

What you get

  • Reduced time-to-hire and cost-per-hire
  • Scalable capacity that flexes with your growth
  • Consistent employer brand experience for every candidate
  • Data-driven insights across your talent pipeline
Best suited forGrowing SMBs, enterprises scaling headcount, and organisations looking to standardise their hiring process.
02 · BPO

Business Process Outsourcing

"Focus on what matters. We handle the rest."Get started

Our BPO services let you delegate non-core business functions to a trusted partner, freeing your internal teams to focus on strategy and growth. From back-office operations to customer support and process management, ONE9 delivers quality execution at scale.

What you get

  • Operational efficiency through process optimisation
  • Cost reduction without sacrificing quality
  • Dedicated teams aligned to your workflows and standards
  • Seamless integration with your existing systems
Best suited forBusinesses seeking to reduce operational costs, improve process consistency, and free up leadership bandwidth.
03 · IT Recruitment

IT Recruitment

"The right tech talent, precisely placed."Get started

Finding skilled technology professionals is one of the hardest challenges in today's market. ONE9 brings deep networks, technical understanding and a rigorous matching process to source and place IT talent, from software engineers and architects to data scientists, DevOps specialists and CXOs.

What you get

  • Access to a curated network of vetted tech professionals
  • Roles filled across all seniority levels and specialisations
  • Contract, permanent and project-based placements
  • Fast turnaround without compromising on fit
Best suited forStartups building their first engineering team, enterprises scaling tech functions, and organisations with niche or hard-to-fill IT roles.

We also deliver offshoring, captive/EOR and managed operations. Security, confidentiality and compliance are fundamental to every engagement, with delivery aligned to the regulatory, contractual and client-specific requirements that apply to you.
